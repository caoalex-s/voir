# Third-party notices

## Batlow / Scientific colour maps

The frozen `data/batlow10.mat` palette and copies embedded in original MAT workspaces derive from Fabio Crameri's Scientific colour maps. The repository preserves the supplied ten-row values. The exact upstream release and the original palette acquisition record were not retained; no version-specific identity is claimed.

Source and citation: Fabio Crameri, *Scientific colour maps*, Zenodo, [10.5281/zenodo.1243862](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1243862); [author project page](https://www.fabiocrameri.ch/colourmaps/).

The colour maps are distributed under the MIT licence. The complete notice below preserves the Crameri copyright and permission text published with the colour-map data in the [cmcrameri distribution](https://github.com/callumrollo/cmcrameri/blob/main/LICENSE.txt).
